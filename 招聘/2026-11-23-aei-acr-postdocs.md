# AEI Potsdam：引力波科学博士后职位（多个）

[返回招聘列表](README.md)

- **机构：** Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics（Albert Einstein Institute, AEI），Potsdam
- **部门：** Astrophysical and Cosmological Relativity（ACR）
- **职位：** 多个引力波科学博士后职位，级别根据经验与资历确定
- **充分考虑截止日期：** 2026 年 11 月 23 日（推荐信也须在此前收到）
- **预计入职：** 2027 年秋季
- **聘期：** 2–5 年
- **完整公告与申请说明：** <https://www.aei.mpg.de/1513474/acr-postdoc-2027>

## 信息摘要

AEI Potsdam 的 ACR 部门招聘多名引力波科学方向博士后，岗位级别将根据申请人的经验和资历确定，聘期为 2–5 年。部门希望申请人具有创造力和高度积极性，既能独立开展研究，也能在大型跨学科团队中合作。建议尽早申请；为获得充分考虑，完整申请材料及推荐信须在 2026 年 11 月 23 日前提交。此后申请仍会继续审议，直至所有岗位招满。

## 完整通知原文

Dear LISA Consortium members,

The "Astrophysical and Cosmological Relativity" (ACR) department at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute, AEI) in Potsdam invites applications for several postdoctoral appointments in gravitational-wave science, offered at different levels according to experience and seniority, with durations from two to five years. We are looking for creative, highly motivated researchers who work equally well independently and within a large, interdisciplinary group.

Candidates are encouraged to apply as early as possible. The deadline for full consideration is 23 November 2026, including receipt of reference letters. Applications will be considered until all positions are filled. The anticipated start date of the positions is Fall 2027.

The complete announcement and instructions how to apply can be found here:

<https://www.aei.mpg.de/1513474/acr-postdoc-2027>

Best wishes,

Jan Steinhoff (on behalf of the ACR department @ AEI)

> 申请前请以招聘机构的完整公告为准。
