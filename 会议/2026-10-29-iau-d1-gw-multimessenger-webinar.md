## 活动简介

本次全球在线报告邀请十二位青年科学家，内容覆盖完整的引力波频谱，包括致密天体族群、脉冲星计时、LISA、强引力透镜和多信使天文学。活动由 IAU Commission D1 志愿组织，旨在分享最新科研成果，并促进不同大洲、观测设施、频段和研究传统之间的联系。活动还将介绍 IAU 与 Commission D1 的工作，以及青年科研人员申请成为 IAU Junior Member 或 Individual Member 并参与委员会和工作组的方式。

## 通知原文

The Organizing Committee of IAU Commission D1 — Gravitational Wave Astrophysics, a branch of IAU Division D — *High-energy Phenomena and Fundamental Physics*, is pleased to invite you to attend the worldwide webinar:

***Gravitational Waves and Multimessenger Astronomy***

**to be held across 29–30 October 2026.**

The webinar will bring together twelve early-career scientists working across the full gravitational-wave spectrum, from compact-object populations and pulsar timing to LISA, strong lensing and multimessenger science.

This webinar is a voluntary initiative of Commission D1, organized under the auspices of the IAU. Its aim is not only to present exciting new scientific results, but also to forge connections across continents, observatories, frequency bands and research traditions.

During the webinar, we will briefly introduce the activities of the IAU and Commission D1 and explain how early-career researchers can become IAU Junior or Individual Members and join the Commission. Membership provides an opportunity to contribute to an international scientific community, participate in Commissions and Working Groups, help identify emerging scientific priorities, develop new initiatives, and contribute to shaping future IAU Symposia and Focus Meetings.

We encourage you to attend!

Those interested can find the announcement and registration at the following link:

<https://golamshaifullah.github.io/iau-d1-webinar/>
